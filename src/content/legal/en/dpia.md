*A summary of Kumpa's data protection impact assessment of 17 November 2026.*

Kumpa handles things most people keep private: that you use an app for meeting people, whom you are looking for, and what you write to a match. Before a service like this opens, the law asks it to work out what could go wrong for the people who use it (GDPR, Article 35). We did. The full assessment is an internal document that the Data Protection Ombudsman can ask to see. This is its summary.

## What we looked at

Everything the app, our server and our moderators do with your data, from sign-in to deletion, and the companies that process data for us. They are listed in the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy/).

## The main risks we found

- Someone you were shown to tells others, or takes a screenshot.
- A data breach reveals who uses Kumpa and whom they seek.
- Someone works out roughly where you live.
- Harassment and unwanted pictures.
- An automatic check gets it wrong: a photo is refused or an account is limited by mistake.

## What we do about them

- We never store your personal identity code, your name or your date of birth. Your bank confirms that you are a real adult, and we keep only your year and month of birth and an identifier that cannot be turned back into your identity code.
- Your home is kept as an area of at least 3,000 people, and distance is shown only as a rough band.
- Whom you seek is used only with your explicit consent and is never written on your profile. Being shown to someone does tell them that you might be interested in people like them, and the same is true the other way round.
- Everyone is identified through their bank, so a person who misuses what they see can be held to account. You can report and block from any profile or conversation.
- Notifications never say who or what.
- No advertising, no data sold or shared for research, no public profiles.
- When you ask, a person reviews an automatic photo decision, unless the check found nudity with very high confidence. A moderator reviews every automatic limit on an account.
- A person whose account is limited is told why, and how to contest it.

## What is still open

- The automatic check of pictures sent in conversations touches the confidentiality of messages. A lawyer has not looked at this yet.

## Our conclusion

Our assessment is that, with these measures in place and the ones planned before opening done, the remaining risk is not high, so the Data Protection Ombudsman does not need to be consulted before Kumpa opens. We go through the assessment again whenever we change how personal data is handled.
