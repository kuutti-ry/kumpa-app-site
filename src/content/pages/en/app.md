## How Kumpa works

In Kumpa you choose who you want to meet. If you both like each other, you can start a conversation.

Kumpa is in closed beta. If you’d like to help test it, email us at [hello@kumpa.fi](mailto:hello@kumpa.fi).

### Make a profile

Sign in with your Finnish bank ID. This confirms that you’re at least 18 and that you have just one account. You choose the name others see. Add your photos, tell people a little about yourself and set your preferences.

### Meet people at your own pace

Kumpa shows one profile at a time. You’ll see people who already like you first. Tap **Like** or **Pass**, or choose **Later** to save someone to a list only you can see. You can keep browsing until you’ve seen everyone who fits. There’s a daily limit on likes.

### When you match

You can send each other messages and decide when to share contact details. If someone makes you uncomfortable, you can block or report them from their profile or your conversation.

Need a hand? [Get help with Kumpa](/help/), read our [child safety standards](/child-safety/) or see [how to delete your account](/delete-account/).
