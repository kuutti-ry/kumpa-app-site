Write to [hello@kuutti.app](mailto:hello@kuutti.app) to reach the volunteers behind Kumpa. We welcome questions about the app, requests about your personal data and offers to help.

## Interviews and demos

Journalists can reach us at the same address. Our [media kit](/about/media/) has the facts about Kumpa and links to source material.

## App support

[Help](/help/) covers sign-in and reporting. You can also [request account deletion](/delete-account/) by email if you can’t use the app.
