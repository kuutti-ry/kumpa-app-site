If something goes wrong in Kumpa, email [hello@kumpa.fi](mailto:hello@kumpa.fi).

## I can’t register

You register once, with your Finnish bank ID. After that, Kumpa keeps you signed in, and you can lock the app with a passcode, your face or your fingerprint. You only identify yourself with your bank ID again if you sign out or reinstall the app, or if the Kumpa team asks you to.

If your bank’s identification page fails, try again later. If Kumpa refuses your registration, the app tells you why. You must be at least 18. If you recently deleted an account, the app shows when you can open a new one.

## I want to report someone

You can report a profile, photo or conversation in the app. Moderators review reports within 24 hours. Blocking someone stops you from seeing each other. Read our [child safety standards](/child-safety/).

## I want a copy of my data

Open **Settings**, choose **Your account**, then **Download my data**. You can also [delete your account](/delete-account/) there.
