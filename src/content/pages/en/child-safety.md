Kumpa is a dating app for adults, published by Kuutti ry, a registered Finnish non-profit association. Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) is prohibited in Kumpa, in every form. These are the standards the association holds Kumpa and its users to.

## Adults only

Nobody under 18 can have an account. Every person signs in with a Finnish bank's identification, which gives the person's age, and the sign-in refuses anyone under 18. There is no other way to make an account, and no account without an identified adult behind it.

Nobody in Kumpa can ask to be shown people under 18: the youngest age anyone can seek is 18.

## What is prohibited

It is prohibited to use Kumpa to:

- make, publish, send, ask for or link to child sexual abuse material (CSAM), in a photo, a text or a message, whether it shows a real child or is drawn or generated;
- seek contact with anyone under 18, or use Kumpa to reach a child through somebody else;
- groom, sexualise, threaten or extort a child, or arrange the abuse or the trafficking of a child;
- let a person under 18 use your account.

The [terms of use](/legal/terms/) say the same in sections 3 and 4: an account is for one adult, and seeking contact with anyone under 18 and publishing or sending unlawful content are breaches of the terms.

## How to report

In the app, a person, a photo and a conversation can each be reported where they are shown, without leaving the app. Moderators of the association review the reports.

You can also write to [hello@kuutti.app](mailto:hello@kuutti.app). You do not need an account to do so.

If a child is in immediate danger, call the emergency number, 112 in Finland. A crime against a child can always be reported to the police directly.

## What Kuutti ry does

Every photo is checked by automated means before anyone else sees it, in a profile and in a conversation.

When the association learns of child sexual abuse material in Kumpa, or of a user who seeks or exploits a child, it:

- removes the material;
- closes the account for good. An account is a bank-identified person, so the person cannot open a new one;
- reports the matter to the Finnish police, and answers the lawful requests of the authorities that investigate it.

Kuutti ry follows the child safety laws that apply to it, Finland's first of all.
