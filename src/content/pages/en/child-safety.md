---
title: "Child safety standards"
description: "Kumpa’s standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation and how to report a concern."
---

Kumpa is for adults. Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) are prohibited in every form. You can report a concern in the app or email [hello@kumpa.fi](mailto:hello@kumpa.fi), even without an account.

## Adults only

Everyone must verify their identity with Finnish bank credentials. Anyone under 18 is refused an account. The youngest age a user can seek is 18.

## What is prohibited

- Creating, sharing, requesting or linking to child sexual abuse material, including drawings and generated material.
- Seeking contact with anyone under 18, directly or through another person.
- Grooming, sexualising, threatening or extorting a child, or arranging the abuse or trafficking of a child.
- Letting anyone under 18 use your account.

These rules also form part of the [terms of use](/legal/terms/).

Your bank ID is yours alone: Finnish law forbids lending it to anyone, and using someone else’s can be identity theft.

## Reporting and response

Report a profile, photo or conversation where you see it in the app. Moderators review every report within 24 hours.

Kumpa checks photos automatically before others can see them, including photos sent in a conversation. When we learn about child sexual abuse material or a person using Kumpa to seek out or exploit a child, we remove the material, permanently close the account and report the matter to Finnish police. We respond to lawful requests from investigating authorities and follow applicable child safety laws.

If a child is in immediate danger in Finland, call **112**. You can also report a crime directly to the police.
